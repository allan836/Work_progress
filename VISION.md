# Career Vision: High-Value Platform Engineer

**Current Role:** Cloud and DevOps Engineer  
**Target Role:** High-Value Platform Engineer  
**Company:** Tech4nl  
**Start Date:** July 1, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 9, 2026

---

## 🎯 Vision Statement

To become a **high-value platform engineer** who builds and maintains platforms that deliver exceptional value to developers and end-users through:

- **Ease of Use**: Intuitive, well-documented, accessible platforms
- **Speed**: Fast deployment, quick onboarding, rapid development cycles
- **Safety**: Secure by default, minimal attack surface, compliant
- **Reliability**: High availability, self-healing, predictable performance

---

## 🌟 Core Principles

### Developer Experience First
- Reduce friction in development workflows
- Automate repetitive tasks
- Provide clear, actionable feedback
- Enable self-service capabilities

### Platform as Product
- Treat internal platforms as products with users (developers)
- Measure and improve platform adoption
- Iterate based on user feedback
- Document value delivered (time saved, costs reduced, incidents prevented)

### Proactive Operations
- Monitor before users complain
- Detect and resolve issues automatically where possible
- Build observability into everything
- Create runbooks and playbooks for common scenarios

### Cost Consciousness
- Optimize for value, not just for technology
- Reduce unnecessary SaaS/licensing costs
- Right-size infrastructure
- Make cost visibility part of the platform

**Vision:** A single, unified platform where developers can:

- Monitor and troubleshoot with integrated observability

**Components:**
